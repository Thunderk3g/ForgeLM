## E1. Student ladder on Banking77

**Question.** With 8, 32 or all labels per class, which LLM-free student is most accurate, how well calibrated is it, and what does it cost on a CPU?

**Setup.** 77 banking intents, 3080 test texts. 385 training texts (5 per class) are held out to fit the confidence temperature and are never trained on. Few-shot rows are the mean ± standard deviation over 3 random draws; full-data rows are one run. No hyper-parameter search: the linear-head students use library defaults, and the fine-tuned encoder uses settings fixed in advance (learning rate 1e-4, batch 32, 64 tokens, 30 / 12 / 5 epochs), so it could score higher with tuning.
